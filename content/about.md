## AI and this blog

I use AI as a writing tool, and every post says how much. At the end of each one you'll find one of three labels:

- **No AI** (`none`): written without AI assistance.
- **AI-assisted** (`ai-assisted`): I wrote it, and AI helped with editing and wording.
- **AI-generated** (`ai-generated`): AI drafted it from my measurements and notes, then I reviewed it and checked every number and command.

Whatever the label, the measurements, benchmarks and commands are mine, and I check every number before publishing. The raw data and scripts sit next to each post so you can check them too. Each post also carries its label as a `<meta name="ai-disclosure">` tag, following the [AI Content Disclosure for HTML](https://github.com/dweekly/ai-content-disclosure) proposal.

You're welcome to find these posts through search engines and through AI assistants that link back here. I don't want them used to train AI models. The site's `robots.txt` says so with `ai-train=no` and blocks the known training crawlers, and the site reserves text and data mining rights under EU law with [TDMRep](https://www.w3.org/community/reports/tdmrep/CG-FINAL-tdmrep-20240510/).
